Malapascua, Cebu

A small island off northern Cebu, with the beach a short walk away.

Starlight sits in Barangay Logon so you can sleep near Bounty Beach and still be across from the market. Use this page to plan the boats, the dives, and the walks.

Bounty Beach

The main sandy beach is about five to ten minutes on foot. Most dive shops and many restaurants line that walk. Logon Church and the ferry landing are also a short walk from the resort gate.

Guimbitayan Beach is farther along the island. Bagacay Point Lighthouse is a short ride across Malapascua, on the order of a mile from the resort.

Thresher sharks, booked with a dive shop

Monad Shoal, off the island, is the early-morning dive Malapascua is known for. Shops along the path to Bounty Beach run those trips. Starlight does not run the boats. Come back to a private bathroom and a hot shower after the dive.

Before dawn

Thresher shark trips usually leave early. Tell the resort if you need a quiet check-in the night before.

Walkable

French Kiss, Evolution, and other shops are a few minutes toward the beach. Pick the shop yourself.

Not a dive center

The resort is gated lodging with a garden, lounge, and snack bar. Diving is next door, not in the rate.

From Cebu

Airport, north road, Maya boat.

  1. Land at Mactan-Cebu International Airport. Cebu City is on the way north, not on the island. The airport is roughly 70 miles from Malapascua by road and sea.
  2. Take a bus or van from the Cebu north bus terminal toward Maya Port in Daanbantayan. Leave time. The ride is often three to four hours, and you still need daylight for the boat.
  3. Buy a passenger-boat ticket at Maya. The crossing is often thirty to forty-five minutes. Boats stop when the wind and swell are too much, including in typhoon weather.
  4. On the island, walk or take a short tricycle ride to Barangay Logon. The resort gate faces the public market.

Some listings mention an airport shuttle for a fee. Ask the resort before you pay a driver, so you know the current price and the meeting point.
