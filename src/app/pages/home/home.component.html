The island

Sleep here. Dive with a shop down the path.

Malapascua is known for dawn trips to Monad Shoal, where thresher sharks are often seen. Starlight is not a dive center. It is the gated room you walk back to after the boat, about five minutes from Bounty Beach.

Getting here

Cebu airport, then the boat from Maya.

  1. Fly into Mactan-Cebu International Airport.
  2. Travel north to Maya Port in Daanbantayan. The road often takes three to four hours.
  3. Take a daytime boat to Malapascua, about thirty to forty-five minutes when the sea is calm.
  4. Walk to Barangay Logon. The resort gate faces the public market.
