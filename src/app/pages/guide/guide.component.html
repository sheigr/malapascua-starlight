Around the property

Published listings describe a fully fenced, gated property with a garden, a shared lounge, and a snack bar. Guests also mention laundry, a 24-hour desk, luggage storage, and help with tours. Breakfast, massage, and an airport shuttle show up as paid extras. Wi-Fi is listed for the public areas. Ask which of these are open when you reserve.
